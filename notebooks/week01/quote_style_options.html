Proposed box grammar

Box: bounded content

Use only when two things must be read as a unit: figure + interpretation, or a revealed set of model assumptions.

Marker: an action

Discussion, modelling choice, and ladder movement use their symbols and a short label. No surrounding box.

Open space: ideas

Quotes, conclusions, transitions, references, and emphasis remain unboxed. Hierarchy comes from scale, spacing, and one accent.
