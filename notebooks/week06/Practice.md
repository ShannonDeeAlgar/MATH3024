# Practice questions

1. Distinguish phase synchronisation, frequency entrainment and phase locking. Give an example in which two of these occur without the third.
2. For two uncoupled oscillators, derive the evolution of their phase difference. What changes after sinusoidal coupling is introduced?
3. Explain every term in the Kuramoto model, including how $\omega_i$ is initialised and what all-to-all coupling means.
4. Starting from $r e^{i\psi}=N^{-1}\sum_j e^{i\theta_j}$, explain geometrically why $r\approx0$ indicates cancellation and $r\approx1$ indicates coherence.
5. Natural frequencies are drawn from an increasingly broad distribution while $K$ is fixed. Predict and then explain the effect on long-time coherence, time to synchrony and the number of drifting oscillators.
6. Design an ensemble experiment across $K$. Choose a centre and spread summary, justify the number of runs, and state how transients are removed.
7. Fireflies flash in physical space, while standard Kuramoto oscillators do not occupy a spatial domain. Identify what the model retains, omits and would need to add for spatial visibility.
8. Write one calculation question, one interpretation question and one model-criticism question. Exchange them with another student, answer theirs, and revise any question that admits an unintended interpretation.
