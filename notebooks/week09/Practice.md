# Practice questions

1. Define entropy, joint entropy, conditional entropy and mutual information, and derive their relationships from the information diagram.
2. State when entropy is zero and evaluate $I(X;X)$.
3. Compare the entropy of uniform and non-uniform alphabets. Can different distributions have the same entropy?
4. Use letter frequencies to estimate the entropy of English text and compare authors or writing styles.
5. Construct efficient yes/no questions for identifying a letter. How does the strategy change when letters are not equally likely?
6. Let the joint probabilities of two binary variables be $p(0,0)=0.40$, $p(0,1)=0.10$, $p(1,0)=0.20$ and $p(1,1)=0.30$. Calculate the marginal, joint and conditional entropies and the mutual information.
7. Calculate permutation entropy for short sequences and investigate how it changes with ordinal-pattern probabilities and embedding length.
8. Identify an information measure relevant to your project system and explain what variable and scale it would be calculated over.
9. Two datasets have the same Shannon entropy but visibly different structure. Construct an example and explain the limitation this exposes.
10. Estimate entropy from samples of increasing length. What changes because of the system, and what changes only because of finite sampling?
11. Compare entropy, mutual information and transfer entropy for a leader–follower system. Which claims can each support, and which causal claim remains unsafe?
12. A continuous variable must be binned before discrete entropy is calculated. Show how the result depends on bin width and state how you would report that choice.
